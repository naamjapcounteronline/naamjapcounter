Why devotees chant the name of Radha

In the Vaishnava traditions of Braj, Shri Radha is revered as the supreme devotee of Krishna and the embodiment of pure, selfless love. Many devotees say that to remember Radha is to remember Krishna with all the love of the heart. That is why the greeting of Vrindavan and Barsana is simply "Radhe Radhe", and why countless people begin and end their day with this one name.

A Radha naam jap counter helps you turn that love into a steady practice. Instead of wondering whether you did 100 or 108 repetitions, the counter keeps the number while you keep the name on your lips and in your mind. It is especially helpful for busy people who chant in short bursts: on the commute, in the kitchen, or before sleep.

How to use this Radha name counter

  1. Sit comfortably and take a few slow breaths. If you like, light a diya and keep an image of Radha-Krishna in front of you.
  2. Choose your name. Popular choices are "Radhe Radhe", "Shri Radhe" and "Jai Shri Radhe". Use the same one throughout your session.
  3. Set a sankalp from the target menu: 27, 54, 108, 216, 540 or 1008. Beginners do well with 108.
  4. Tap once per name. You can chant aloud, in a whisper or silently in the mind; the counter works the same for all three. Space or Enter also counts on a keyboard, and Backspace undoes a mistaken tap.
  5. Listen for the bell. At the end of every 108 the counter chimes softly and your round number increases.
  6. End with gratitude. Sit for a minute, offer your japa to Radha Rani and bow.

Special days for Radha naam japa

Radha Ashtami, the appearance day of Shri Radha, falls on the eighth day (ashtami) of the bright half of the month of Bhadrapada, about two weeks after Krishna Janmashtami. Devotees fast, sing bhajans and chant Radha naam all day. Ekadashi, Purnima and the days of the Kartik month are also loved by devotees for extended naam japa. Check a local panchang for exact dates in your city.

Tips to make Radha naam jap a daily habit

  • Start with 5 minutes. A calm 108 every day is more powerful than a rushed 1008 once a month.
  • Pair it with an existing habit such as brushing your teeth, tea, or putting your phone on charge.
  • Use the streak and daily goal in the counter. Watching a seven-day bar chart fill up is genuinely motivating.
  • Chant with feeling. Imagine you are calling out to a beloved friend rather than reciting a number.
  • Forgive missed days. Simply begin again; the name never goes away.

Radha naam: tradition, pronunciation and daily method

Where it is honoured: Radha is central in the devotional traditions of Vrindavan and Barsana, in the Brahma Vaivarta Purana and in the Vaishnava stotras that glorify her, such as the Radha Kripa Kataksha Stotra. In Braj, "Radhe Radhe" is a daily greeting as well as a mantra.

Pronunciation: "RAA-dhay", the first vowel long and the "dh" soft. "Shri Radhe" is "shree RAA-dhay".

Method: Common practice is 108 repetitions of "Radhe Radhe" or "Shri Radhe", often in the morning and on Ekadashi, Radha Ashtami and Kartik. Because the name is short, it is easy to match to the breath: one name per exhale.

What devotees say: the name brings closeness to Krishna through his own beloved. This is a devotional teaching and not a promise of results. See also Radha Rani ke naam.

Frequently asked questions

How many times should I chant Radha naam?

There is no fixed rule. Many devotees chant 108 times (one mala) daily and increase to 540 or 1008 on special days. Choose a number you can complete with attention and keep it steady.

Can I chant Radhe Radhe silently?

Yes. Silent or mental chanting (manasika japa) is considered very subtle. You can tap the counter once per repetition without making any sound.

Is this Radha naam jap counter free?

Yes, it is completely free with no login. Your counts are saved only in your own browser.

What is the difference between Radha naam and Radha Krishna chanting?

Radha naam focuses on the name of Shri Radha alone, while "Radha Krishna" or "Radhe Shyam" invokes the divine couple together. You can choose whichever your tradition or heart prefers; the counter works for any of them using the custom chip.

Can I use this radha jap in count on my phone?
