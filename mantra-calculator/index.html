Mantra Calculator – Plan Your Japa Sankalp

Use this mantra calculator to see how long your daily japa takes and how many days you need to finish a sankalp of 1 lakh, 1.25 lakh, 11 lakh or any number.

Mantra calculator – time & total chants

How the mantra calculator works

Enter your daily malas, chants per round (108 by default), seconds per chant and your total sankalp. The calculator multiplies malas by chants to get your daily total, converts it to time, and divides the sankalp by the daily total to give the number of days.

Formula: days = total sankalp ÷ (malas per day × chants per mala). Time per day = chants per day × seconds per chant.

Popular sankalps

  • Sawa lakh (1.25 lakh): often done as a purashcharana unit for some mantras.
  • 11 lakh, 21 lakh: larger anushthans. Traditional methods may include specific rules; please consult a teacher.
  • Ek mala daily for 40 days (4,320): a beginner's rhythm for forming a habit.
  • Navratri 9-day japa: choose the daily number such that nine days complete your target.

Planning tips

  1. Use a conservative daily number you can do even on busy days.
  2. Choose a fixed time, ideally in Brahma Muhurta.
  3. Track progress with the japa counter.
  4. Add a day or two as a buffer for missed days.

Frequently asked questions

How many days does it take to chant 1.25 lakh mantras?

At 11 malas (1,188 chants) a day it takes about 106 days; at 21 malas a day about 56 days.

How long does one mala take?

At about 3 seconds per chant, one mala of 108 takes around 5 to 6 minutes.

How many chants in a lakh?

One lakh is 100,000 chants, which is roughly 926 malas of 108.

Can I use a number other than 108?

Yes. Change "Chants per round" to any number such as 54 or 27.

Is the mantra calculator free?

Yes, free with no login.
