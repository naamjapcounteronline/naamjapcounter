One counter, many mantras

People who search for a mantra counter usually follow more than one practice. You may chant Gayatri in the morning, Om Namah Shivaya on Mondays and your guru mantra in the evening. A single global counter would mix them all together. This page stores a separate lifetime total, today's total and round position for every mantra.

How to add your own mantra

  1. Select the Custom chip.
  2. Type the mantra or name exactly as you chant it – in Devanagari, Gurmukhi, Tamil or romanised text.
  3. Tap Save. The text appears above the ring and its count is stored under "Custom".

This is useful for guru-given mantras, family deity names or beej mantras that are not in the list.

What a "mantra count" really means

Traditions differ: for some, one mantra equals one complete recitation; for others, each name or word is one count. Pick one method and use it consistently. For long verses, count each full recitation; for short names, each name. The mantra calculator can convert a target such as 1,25,000 into daily malas and days.

Who should use the mantra counter page?

  • Practitioners who change mantra by day of the week or deity.
  • Devotees with a guru-given mantra not covered by presets.
  • Teachers who need to give a class a simple counter for any mantra.

Frequently asked questions

What is a mantra counter?

A mantra counter counts how many times you repeat a mantra, and on this site it saves a separate total for each mantra.

Can I count my own guru mantra?

Yes. Choose Custom, type the mantra and save it. Counting works exactly like the built-in mantras.

Are counts mixed between mantras?

No. Each mantra has its own lifetime total and round position; the daily total adds them together.

Can I count 1,25,000 repetitions for a purascharana?

Yes. Use the mantra calculator to split it into daily malas, then track each day here.

Does it work in Hindi, Tamil or Punjabi text?

Yes. Custom text can be in any language your device keyboard supports.
