Jaap kya hai aur jaap counter kyun?

Jaap (जाप) ka matlab hai kisi pavitra naam ya mantra ko baar-baar dohrana. Ghar mein dadi-nani se lekar mandir ke pujari tak, sab "naam jaap" karte hain – Ram, Radhe, Shiv, Hanuman ya Waheguru. Mala ke bina ginti yaad rakhna mushkil hota hai, isliye ek jaap counter madad karta hai: aap sirf naam par dhyan dein, ginti counter rakhe.

English readers: what is jaap?

"Jaap", "jap" and "japa" are spellings of one practice: repeating a divine name with attention. This counter treats each tap as one repetition and each 108 as one round, so it works for every tradition. Compare with the mantra counter (any mantra), the digital mala (bead visual) and the chanting counter (wider terminology).
