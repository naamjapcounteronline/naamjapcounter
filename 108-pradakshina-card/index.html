108 Pradakshina Card – Mark Each Parikrama

Walking 108 pradakshinas? This 108 pradakshina card lets you tap each number as you complete a round, saves progress in your browser and prints cleanly.

108 pradakshina card

Tap a number after each parikrama (circumambulation). Progress is saved on your device. Gold-bordered boxes mark 27, 54, 81 and 108.

What is pradakshina?

Pradakshina (also parikrama) is the devotional practice of walking clockwise around a sacred object, such as a deity, temple, tree (for example peepal or tulsi), river or mountain. The right side is kept towards the object to show reverence. Many people take a vow of 11, 21, 51 or 108 pradakshinas for a wish, a thanksgiving or a festival.

How to use the card

  1. Stand at your starting point and make a short prayer or sankalp.
  2. Walk one round slowly and quietly, chanting a mantra in the mind.
  3. Tap the next number on the card after each round.
  4. Gold-bordered boxes mark 27, 54, 81 and 108 as milestones.
  5. Your progress is saved on your device until you reset or clear data.

Guidelines

  • Walk clockwise only, with calm steps.
  • Do not push others in crowded temples; follow local rules.
  • If you cannot walk long, split the 108 over several days or do mental pradakshina.
  • Elders and people with health issues should avoid overexertion.

Chants to say while walking

Choose a simple mantra such as Om Namah Shivaya, Ram Ram or Radhe Radhe. You can also use the tap counter in your pocket in parallel for the chanting count.

Frequently asked questions

What is a 108 pradakshina card?

It is a sheet with numbers 1 to 108 that you mark after each pradakshina so that you do not lose count.

Can I print the card?

Yes. Use the print button or your browser print menu; the card prints without menus and ads.

Do I have to do 108 in one day?

Not necessarily. Many people split a vow over several days. Follow your family or temple tradition.

Is pradakshina always clockwise?

Yes, in Hindu tradition it is clockwise, keeping the sacred object on your right.
