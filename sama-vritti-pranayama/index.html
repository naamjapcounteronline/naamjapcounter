Sama Vritti Pranayama – Guided Equal Breathing

Sama vritti pranayama is the yogic practice of equal-length breathing. Follow the glowing circle to inhale, hold, exhale and hold for the same count, then begin your japa with a calm mind.

Sama vritti pranayama (box breathing)

Inhale, hold, exhale, hold – all for the same count. Practise 2–5 minutes before your naam jap to settle the mind.

What is sama vritti pranayama?

"Sama" means equal and "vritti" means fluctuation or pattern. In sama vritti pranayama you keep the inhalation and exhalation equal in length. A common version, also known in modern settings as box breathing, adds equal holds after inhalation and exhalation, making a four-sided "box" of breath: inhale 4, hold 4, exhale 4, hold 4.

How to practise (step by step)

  1. Sit comfortably with a long spine and relaxed shoulders.
  2. Exhale completely through the nose.
  3. Inhale slowly for four counts.
  4. Hold gently for four counts (no strain).
  5. Exhale slowly for four counts.
  6. Hold gently for four counts.
  7. Repeat for 2 to 5 minutes. Use the animated circle above to keep time.
Never strain. If you feel dizzy, stop and breathe normally. Beginners can skip the holds and simply do 4-in, 4-out.

Benefits commonly reported

  • A feeling of calm and focus.
  • A steadier pace of breathing, which can ease the nervous tension that comes with stress.
  • An easier transition from daily activity into meditation or japa.

This is a gentle relaxation practice, not a medical treatment. Research on slow breathing suggests modest relaxation benefits, but individual effects vary.

Cautions

People with respiratory or heart conditions, high blood pressure, pregnancy or panic disorder should consult a doctor before practising breath retention. Avoid holds if they cause discomfort. Learn advanced pranayama from a qualified teacher.

Combining with naam japa

A short routine: 2 minutes of sama vritti, then chant one mala in the naam jap counter. Many people synchronise a short mantra with the breath: for example "Ra" while inhaling and "m" while exhaling for Ram naam.

Frequently asked questions

What does sama vritti mean?

Sama means equal and vritti means pattern or fluctuation. It refers to breathing in equal-length phases.

Is sama vritti the same as box breathing?

They are very similar. Box breathing is the modern name for equal inhale, hold, exhale, hold, and is often used in stress management.

How long should I practise?

Start with 2 to 3 minutes and increase to 5 to 10 minutes gradually.

Is it safe for everyone?

Generally gentle, but avoid retention if you have medical conditions and consult a doctor first.

Can I do it before japa?

Yes. It is a common way to settle the mind before chanting.
