Radha Rani Ke Naam – 28 Names Of Shri Radha With Meaning

Shri Radha is lovingly addressed by many names. Here are 28 well-known names with their Hindi spelling and meaning, and how to use them in your Radha naam jap.

28 names of Radha Rani

Different sampradayas and saints list different names, and there are famous lists of 28, 32 and 108 names. The list below includes widely used names and epithets; the meanings are traditional glosses and are meant for devotion rather than scholarly translation. Follow the list given by your own tradition or guru.

How to chant the names of Radha

  1. Select Radha Naam in the Radha naam jap counter.
  2. Chant "Radhe Radhe" or choose one of the names above with the Custom chip.
  3. Tap once per repetition.
  4. On Radha Ashtami, chant more than usual with love.

Radha-related mantras

  • Radhe Radhe – the Braj greeting and simplest chant.
  • Shri Radhe Krishna – invoking the couple.
  • Jai Shri Radhe – a loving victory call.
  • Hare Krishna maha-mantra – where "Hare" is traditionally understood as a call to Radha. See the Hare Krishna counter.

Writing Radha naam

You can also write names in a notebook as likhit japa. A printable sheet of "राधे राधे" × 108 is on the Radha naam counter page.

Frequently asked questions

How many names does Radha have?

Traditional lists include 28, 32, 108 and more. This page shares 28 widely used names.

What does Radha mean?

Radha is commonly explained as "one who worships" or "success"; devotionally she is the supreme devotee of Krishna.

Which name of Radha is best to chant?

The simplest and most loved is "Radhe Radhe". Choose the name your heart or tradition prefers.

What is Radha Ashtami?

The festival marking Radha's appearance, on the eighth day of the bright half of Bhadrapada.
