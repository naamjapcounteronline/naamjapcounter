About Naam Japa Sadhana

The story and purpose behind naamjapcounter.online

Naam Japa Sadhana is a free online naam japa counter and digital mala built to make daily chanting simpler, calmer and more consistent. Whether you chant Radha, Ram, Om Namah Shivaya, Hare Krishna or any other name of the Divine, our goal is the same: let you focus on the chanting, and let the counter take care of counting.

Why we built this

Keeping count of japa is a very old challenge. A physical mala is beautiful, but it is not always with us, and it cannot remember how many rounds we completed yesterday. Many online mantra counters only add one number at a time. We wanted a complete companion: a glowing 108-bead digital mala, gentle sound and vibration, rounds counted automatically, a daily goal, a streak and the practical tools that devotees keep searching for, such as a Brahma Muhurta calculator and a mantra calculator.

What you will find here

📿 Japa counter online

A 108 japa counter with digital mala beads, sankalp targets, auto japa mode and focus mode.

🕉️ Mantra library

Twelve ready mantras in Devanagari with transliteration, meaning and when they are commonly chanted.

🌅 Sadhana tools

Brahma Muhurta time, mantra calculator, sama vritti pranayama guide, 108 pradakshina card and a 1 to 108 counting chart.

📖 Learning guide

A detailed guide on how to do naam jap, why 108 beads are used, japa rules and building a daily habit.

Our principles

  • Free and simple. The counter and tools are free to use, with no sign-up required.
  • Privacy first. Your counts, streaks and settings stay in your own browser. We do not ask you to create an account. Read our Privacy Policy.
  • Respect for tradition. Mantras and practices belong to living traditions. We share general, widely known information and always encourage you to follow your own guru and sampradaya.
  • Honest claims. Japa is a spiritual practice. We do not promise miracles or medical results, and the site is not a substitute for professional medical advice.
  • Devotion over numbers. A counter is only a helper. A calm 108 with attention is better than a rushed 1008.

Feedback & suggestions

This site grows with the suggestions of its users. If you want a new mantra added, found a mistake in a mantra or a calculation, or have an idea for a feature, please write to us from the Contact page or email brandsfortheheart01@gmail.com.
