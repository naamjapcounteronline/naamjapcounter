Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026 · Applies to naamjapcounter.online

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled when you use naamjapcounter.online (the "Website", "we", "us"), and the choices you have. In short: we do not require accounts, and your japa counts are stored on your own device.

1. Information we do not collect

We do not ask you to register, log in or provide your name, phone number, address or payment details to use the counter and tools. We do not run a user database of your japa activity.

2. Information stored on your device

To make the counter useful, the Website saves the following in your browser's local storage on your own device: your selected mantra, counts, round totals, daily history and streak, daily goal and sankalp target, sound/vibration/theme settings, any custom mantra text you type, your 108 pradakshina card progress, your language preference (Hindi/English toggle) and your audio choices (mantra voice, Om drone and loop mode). If you use the mantra voice, the text is spoken by your own browser's built-in speech feature; depending on your device, your operating system's voice service may process it, which is outside our control. If you install the Website as an app, the browser saves a copy of the pages on your device so they can open offline. This data stays on your device. It is not transmitted to us. You can erase it any time by clearing your browser's site data, or by using the Reset buttons in the tools.

3. Location

If you press "Use my location" in the Brahma Muhurta calculator, your browser asks for permission. The coordinates are used only inside your browser to calculate sunrise and Brahma Muhurta time. We do not receive or store your location. You can instead choose a city or enter sunrise time manually.

4. Messages you send us

The Contact page opens your email app with a pre-filled message. If you email us at brandsfortheheart01@gmail.com, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and we use it only to respond to you and improve the Website. We do not sell or rent it.

5. Technical data and hosting

Like most websites, our hosting provider and network infrastructure may automatically process technical data such as IP address, browser type, device type, pages requested and timestamps in server logs, for security, reliability and troubleshooting.

6. Third-party services

  • Google Fonts: the Website loads fonts from Google Fonts. When the fonts load, your browser connects to Google servers, which may receive your IP address and browser information. See Google's privacy policy for details.
  • Hosting / CDN provider: processes technical data as described above.
  • Web Share: if you use the Share button, your device's own share menu or clipboard is used. We do not receive what you share.

7. Cookies, analytics and advertising

The Website itself does not set tracking cookies; it uses browser local storage for the functions described in section 2. If we later add analytics or advertising services (for example Google Analytics or Google AdSense), those providers may use cookies or similar technologies, and we will update this policy and, where required by law, ask for your consent before they run. You can always control cookies through your browser settings.

8. How we use information

  • To provide and improve the counter, tools and content.
  • To respond to your messages and requests.
  • To keep the Website secure and working.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

9. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information only with service providers that help us run the Website, or if required by law or to protect our rights and users' safety.

10. Data retention and your control

Data in your browser stays until you delete it. Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to handle your request and for reasonable record-keeping. You may ask us to delete an email conversation by writing to us.

11. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the Website, and the site is intended to be served over HTTPS. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Since your counts live on your own device, please also keep your device secure.

12. Children's privacy

The Website is a general devotional tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

13. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, or the GDPR in the EU/UK), you may have rights to access, correct, erase or object to the processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. Because we hold very little personal data, most requests can be handled by clearing your browser data or by emailing us at brandsfortheheart01@gmail.com.

14. Links to other websites

The Website may link to external sites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices; please read their policies.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the latest revision. Continued use of the Website after changes means you accept the updated policy.

16. Contact

For any privacy question, write to brandsfortheheart01@gmail.com or use our Contact page.
