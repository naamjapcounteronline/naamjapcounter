What is Brahma Muhurta?

Brahma Muhurta ("the muhurta of Brahma, the creator") is the auspicious period before dawn. A muhurta is a unit of 48 minutes, so Brahma Muhurta is the muhurta that begins 96 minutes before sunrise and ends at 48 minutes before sunrise (the final muhurta of the night). Ayurveda and yogic texts describe it as a time when the world is quiet, the air is fresh and the mind is naturally clear after sleep.

How Brahma Muhurta is calculated

  1. Find the local sunrise time for the date and location.
  2. Subtract 96 minutes to get the start.
  3. Subtract 48 minutes to get the end.

Example: if sunrise is 6:30 AM, Brahma Muhurta is from 4:54 AM to 5:42 AM. Because sunrise changes through the year and with latitude, the time is different in Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai or Guwahati and varies every day. This page calculates sunrise using standard astronomical formulas (accurate to within a few minutes) for each city.

Why people wake and chant in Brahma Muhurta

  • Traditional texts describe it as best for meditation, japa and study.
  • The environment is calm with little noise or distraction.
  • Many people find it easier to build a daily routine when the day starts with quiet practice.

Scientific evidence on a unique effect of this exact time window is limited; the benefit people usually report is simply the quiet, a regular schedule and a good start to the day. If you cannot wake that early, chant at any time you can do so regularly.

A gentle morning routine for Brahma Muhurta

  1. Sleep early the previous night (aim for 7 to 8 hours).
  2. Wake, drink water and wash your face.
  3. Do 2 to 3 minutes of sama vritti pranayama.
  4. Chant one mala (108) using the naam jap counter.
  5. Sit in silence for a few minutes.

Frequently asked questions

What is Brahma Muhurta time tomorrow?

It depends on your city's sunrise. It starts 96 minutes before sunrise and ends 48 minutes before sunrise. Use the table or calculator on this page for tomorrow's exact times.

How long is Brahma Muhurta?

It is one muhurta, which is 48 minutes long.

Is Brahma Muhurta the same in every city?

No. It depends on sunrise, which changes with the city and the season.

What should I do in Brahma Muhurta?

Traditionally meditation, japa, prayer, pranayama or study. Choose a quiet, gentle practice.

Is it necessary to wake in Brahma Muhurta to do japa?

No. It is considered ideal, but regular japa at any time you can sit quietly is also valuable.
