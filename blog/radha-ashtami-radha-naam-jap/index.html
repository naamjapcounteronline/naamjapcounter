Radha Ashtami 2027 – Date, Vrat Vidhi And How To Do Radha Naam Jap

Radha Ashtami celebrates the appearance of Shri Radha, the supreme devotee of Krishna. Here are the date, the tithi timing, a simple vidhi and a practical plan for Radha naam jap on this day.

When is Radha Ashtami in 2027?

In 2027, Radha Ashtami falls on Wednesday, 8 September. According to Drik Panchang, the Ashtami tithi begins at 11:28 AM on 7 September and ends at 12:44 PM on 8 September (New Delhi time). Because the festival is observed on the day the Ashtami tithi is present at sunrise in the traditional method followed by many communities, 8 September is the day for most devotees. Timings change slightly by city, so check your local temple or panchang.

The festival comes about two weeks after Krishna Janmashtami, on the eighth day of the bright half (Shukla paksha) of the month of Bhadrapada. It is celebrated with special joy at Barsana and Vrindavan, where devotees believe Radha Rani appeared, and in Radha-Krishna temples all over India.

Who is Shri Radha and why is she worshipped?

In the Vaishnava traditions, especially those of Braj, Shri Radha is regarded as the highest expression of devotion (bhakti) and the beloved of Krishna. Many devotees say that one cannot approach Krishna without the grace of Radha, which is why the greeting of Braj is simply "Radhe Radhe". Saints such as Swami Haridas, Hit Harivansh Mahaprabhu and Sri Chaitanya Mahaprabhu and his followers have sung her glory in bhajans and poetry for centuries.

Different sampradayas describe Radha in different ways, so if you belong to a particular tradition, follow its understanding and rituals. What all traditions share is the simple practice of remembering her name with love.

How devotees observe Radha Ashtami

  • Morning bath and sankalp. Devotees wake early, bathe and resolve to spend the day in remembrance of Radha-Krishna.
  • Fasting (vrat). Many keep a fast, from a full nirjala fast to a light phalahar of fruit and milk. Those who are unwell, elderly, pregnant or on medicines should not fast strictly and may simply observe the day with prayer.
  • Puja and abhishek. The deity of Radha-Krishna is bathed, dressed in new clothes and decorated with flowers, and offerings (bhog) such as sweets and fruits are made.
  • Bhajan and kirtan. Community singing of Radha Rani bhajans fills temples and homes.
  • Charity. Feeding people, donating clothes or helping those in need is considered especially auspicious on this day.

A simple Radha Ashtami puja vidhi at home

  1. Clean the puja space and place a picture or murti of Radha-Krishna on a clean cloth.
  2. Light a ghee lamp and incense. Offer fresh flowers, tulsi leaves and a little water.
  3. Say a short prayer in your own words, or recite "Shri Radhe Krishna" three times.
  4. Offer simple bhog such as fruit, milk sweets or panchamrit. Avoid onion and garlic in the food.
  5. Begin your naam jap (see the plan below), then sing or listen to a bhajan.
  6. Close with aarti, share the prasad and offer thanks.

Every household follows its own custom, so adapt this outline to your family tradition.

Radha naam jap on Radha Ashtami – step by step

  1. Choose your time. Early morning, in Brahma Muhurta, is traditional. Evening after aarti is also lovely.
  2. Sit comfortably facing the deity or a picture of Radha-Krishna.
  3. Settle the breath for two minutes with sama vritti pranayama.
  4. Open the Radha naam jap counter, set your target and start chanting "Radhe Radhe" or "Shri Radhe".
  5. Tap once for each name. Aloud, whispered or mental japa are all fine.
  6. When the bell rings at 108, pause for a breath and continue with the next round.
  7. Finish with gratitude. Offer the fruit of your japa to Radha Rani.

Names and mantras for the day

  • Radhe Radhe – the simplest and most loved chant of Braj.
  • Shri Radhe and Jai Shri Radhe – used in kirtan and as greetings.
  • Radhe Krishna and Radhe Shyam – invoking the divine couple.
  • Maha-Mantra in Braj Kirtan: In Gaudiya and Pushtimarg traditions, the vocative "Hare" specifically invokes the internal pleasure potency (Hladini Shakti) of Shri Radha. You can track full 16-round festival vows on our Hare Krishna counter.

You can also read the 28 names of Radha Rani with meanings and use any of them as your japa.

Likhit naam japa: writing Radhe Radhe

Many families observe the day by writing "Radhe Radhe" in a notebook, ideally 108 times or more. Writing slows the mind and has a meditative effect similar to chanting. The Radha naam counter page has a printable sheet of "राधे राधे" written 108 times that you can use as a model.

Tips to make the day meaningful

  • Decide your sankalp the evening before so that you do not make decisions in a rush.
  • Keep your phone on Do Not Disturb during japa, and use the Keep screen on option of the counter.
  • Involve the family: a shared bhajan or a group of 108 names makes children part of the festival.
  • Do not turn the fast into a hardship. Devotion matters more than severity.
  • After the festival, keep going: even 108 names every day carries the feeling of Radha Ashtami through the year.

Frequently asked questions

What is the date of Radha Ashtami in 2027?

Radha Ashtami in 2027 is on Wednesday, 8 September. The Ashtami tithi begins at 11:28 AM on 7 September and ends at 12:44 PM on 8 September in New Delhi.

How many days after Janmashtami is Radha Ashtami?

It falls about 15 days after Krishna Janmashtami, on the eighth day of the bright half of Bhadrapada.

How many times should I chant Radha naam on Radha Ashtami?

There is no fixed number. Many devotees chant 108, 540 or 1,008 times. Choose what you can do with attention.

Can I do Radha naam jap silently?

Yes. Silent or mental chanting is considered subtle and effective. You can tap the counter once per name without making any sound.

Is fasting compulsory on Radha Ashtami?

No. Fasting is a personal choice and varies by tradition. Those with health conditions should not fast strictly.
