Maha Shivaratri 2027 – Date, Nishita Kaal And Om Namah Shivaya Jap

Maha Shivaratri is the great night of Lord Shiva. Here are the 2027 date, the Nishita Kaal timing, an outline of the four-prahar puja and a plan for Om Namah Shivaya jap with a counter.

When is Maha Shivaratri in 2027?

Maha Shivaratri 2027 falls on Saturday, 6 March, with the main night being the night of 6 to 7 March. According to Drik Panchang, the Chaturdashi tithi runs from 12:03 PM IST on 6 March to 1:46 PM IST on 7 March. The special midnight puja time, Nishita Kaal, is about 12:07 AM to 12:56 AM on 7 March in Delhi and about 12:25 AM to 1:13 AM in Mumbai. In Hyderabad it is 12:03 AM to 12:51 AM. Timings change from city to city, so check your local panchang.

The festival falls in the dark half of the month of Phalguna in the purnimanta calendar (called Magha in the amanta calendar used in some regions). It is celebrated all night with worship, fasting and jagaran (night vigil).

What is Maha Shivaratri?

Shivaratri means "the night of Shiva". There is a Masik Shivaratri every month on the Krishna Chaturdashi, but the one in Phalguna is called Maha (great) Shivaratri. Different stories explain its significance: that it commemorates the marriage of Shiva and Parvati, that it is the night when Shiva performed the cosmic Tandava dance, or that it is the night of the first manifestation of the Shiva linga as a pillar of fire. Whichever story one follows, the common thread is staying awake in remembrance of Shiva and turning the mind inward.

The four-prahar puja outline

The night is traditionally divided into four praharas (watches), and many devotees worship Shiva in each. A common outline is:

  1. First prahar (evening): abhishek with milk, offering of bilva leaves and chanting of Om Namah Shivaya.
  2. Second prahar: abhishek with curd, continued mantra japa.
  3. Third prahar (including Nishita Kaal): abhishek with ghee, the most important watch for many traditions.
  4. Fourth prahar (before dawn): abhishek with honey and water, concluding prayers.

Temples publish exact prahar timings, which depend on local sunset and sunrise. Practices differ between traditions, so follow your family or temple custom.

Simple home puja for Maha Shivaratri

  • Bathe, wear clean clothes and clean the space.
  • Place a Shiva linga or picture of Shiva. Offer water, milk, bilva (bael) leaves, white flowers and incense. Avoid offering tulsi to Shiva in most traditions.
  • Light a lamp and sit for japa.
  • Read or listen to the Shiva Chalisa, Rudram or a bhajan.
  • If you stay awake, keep the mind engaged with japa, kirtan or reading rather than just staying up.

Om Namah Shivaya: meaning and use

The five-syllable mantra Na-Mah-Shi-Va-Ya, with Om in front, means "I bow to Shiva, the auspicious one". It appears in the Sri Rudram of the Yajur Veda and is the heart of Shaiva japa. Fasting guidelines and cautions

Many devotees fast on Maha Shivaratri, from a strict nirjala fast to a fruit-and-milk vrat. Typical foods include fruits, milk, sabudana, kuttu or singhare atta and rock salt. Please keep these points in mind:

  • If you are pregnant, elderly, diabetic or on medicines, do not fast strictly. Eat light and observe the day with prayer.
  • Drink enough water if your tradition allows.
  • Break the fast gently the next morning after the puja.
  • Traditions about bhang and similar items are cultural; they are not a part of the spiritual requirement and should be avoided if unwell or driving.

Staying awake and focused (jagaran tips)

  • Sleep a little in the afternoon before the night vigil if you can.
  • Alternate between sitting japa, kirtan and standing aarti so the body does not become stiff.

Other Shiva mantras for Shivaratri

Besides Om Namah Shivaya, many devotees chant the Maha Mrityunjaya mantra and listen to the Shiva Tandava Stotram. Whichever you choose, stay with that mantra during the night so that your count is meaningful.

Frequently asked questions

What is the date of Maha Shivaratri in 2027?

Maha Shivaratri 2027 is on Saturday, 6 March, with the main night on 6 to 7 March.

What is Nishita Kaal?

Nishita Kaal is the midnight period considered most auspicious for Shiva puja. In Delhi in 2027 it is about 12:07 AM to 12:56 AM on 7 March.

How many times should I chant Om Namah Shivaya on Shivaratri?

Many devotees chant 108, 648 or 1,008 times through the night. Choose a number you can sustain with attention.

Do I have to stay awake all night?

Jagaran is traditional but optional. If you cannot, spend as much time as you can in prayer and japa.
