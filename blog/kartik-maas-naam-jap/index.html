Kartik Maas 2026 – Dates, Niyam And A Daily Naam Jap Plan

Kartik is the holiest month for many Vaishnava devotees, a time of lamps, tulsi worship and extra japa. Here are the 2026 dates, key festivals and a gentle naam jap plan you can start today.

By Naam Japa Sadhana · Published November 8, 2026

Quick facts

Kartik (purnimanta, North India)Tuesday, 27 October to Tuesday, 24 November 2026
Karthika Masam (amanta, South India)Tuesday, 10 November to Tuesday, 8 December 2026

Kartik maas dates in 2026

The month of Kartik has two different calendars, so two sets of dates are in use:

  • North India (purnimanta): Kartik runs from 27 October to 24 November 2026, ending on Kartik Purnima.
  • South India and some other regions (amanta): Karthika Masam runs from 10 November to 8 December 2026.

This means that, as of today (8 November 2026), devotees in North India are already in the second week of Kartik, while Karthika Masam begins in the South in two days. Follow the calendar of your region.

Why Kartik is special

The Puranas praise Kartik as the most beloved month of Lord Vishnu and Krishna, and as a month when even small acts of devotion are said to bring great merit. For Vaishnavas it is also called Damodara or Urja month, remembering the childhood pastime in which Mother Yashoda bound Krishna with a rope. In many homes the month is marked by lighting lamps (deepdaan), worshipping tulsi, early morning bathing and more time for prayer. In South India Karthika Masam is dear to Shiva devotees, with lamps lit in temples and Mondays (Karthika Somavaram) observed with fasting.

Simple Kartik niyam (daily rules)

  • Wake early. Many devotees bathe before sunrise during Kartik. If you cannot, simply wash your face and hands and sit for a few minutes.
  • Light a lamp every day, at home in the evening, near the tulsi plant or at the temple.
  • Worship tulsi. Offer water, circumambulate and chant a mantra near the plant. Do not pluck tulsi leaves casually; traditions have rules, such as avoiding Ekadashi and evenings.
  • Eat sattvic food. Some avoid onion and garlic, urad dal and similar items during the month.
  • Increase japa. Add even one extra mala (108) to your usual practice.
  • Give. Donate food, a lamp, a blanket or help someone in need.

Practices differ between communities, and these are suggestions, not compulsions.

Tulsi worship and Tulsi Vivah

Tulsi is regarded as a goddess and a beloved of Vishnu. In Kartik, families water the plant daily, light a lamp near it and sing the tulsi aarti. On Tulsi Vivah the plant is decorated like a bride and symbolically married to Shaligram or Krishna, marking the start of the wedding season. You can add a mala of "Om Namo Bhagavate Vasudevaya" in front of the tulsi on that day.

Health and balance

Early baths in cold weather, fasting and long vigils can be hard on the body, especially for the elderly and those with health issues. It is perfectly fine to adapt the practices: a warm bath, a lighter vrat or a shorter japa still carries the spirit of the month.

Frequently asked questions

When is Kartik month in 2026?

In North India (purnimanta) Kartik runs from 27 October to 24 November 2026. In South India (amanta) Karthika Masam runs from 10 November to 8 December 2026.

When is Dev Uthani Ekadashi in 2026?

Devutthana (Prabodhini) Ekadashi is on 20 November for Smartas and 21 November for Vaishnavas.

When is Kartik Purnima in 2026?

Kartik Purnima and Dev Diwali fall on Tuesday, 24 November 2026.

What should I chant in Kartik maas?

Many chant Radha-Krishna names, Om Namo Bhagavate Vasudevaya or Hare Krishna. In South India Shiva mantras are also popular during Karthika Masam.

How many times should I chant in Kartik?

There is no fixed number. Add one extra mala to your usual practice, or follow the 30-day plan above.
