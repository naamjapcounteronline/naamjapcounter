Ekadashi Vrat Niyam And Naam Jap – Upcoming Dates And A Simple Guide

Ekadashi, the eleventh day of each lunar fortnight, is dear to Vishnu devotees. Learn the vrat rules, what to eat, how to break the fast and how to use the day for naam jap.

By Naam Jap Counter · Published November 8, 2026

What is Ekadashi?

Ekadashi means "eleventh". It is the eleventh tithi of both the waxing (Shukla) and waning (Krishna) halves of the lunar month, so it comes roughly twice a month, about 24 times a year. Each Ekadashi has its own name and story in the Puranas, and the day is dedicated to Lord Vishnu and his avatars such as Krishna and Ram. Devotees observe a fast, increase their prayer and japa and try to keep the mind calm and truthful throughout the day.

Basic rules of Ekadashi vrat

  • Avoid grains and beans. Rice, wheat, pulses and foods made from them are avoided in most traditions. Many also avoid onion, garlic and non-vegetarian food as always.
  • Eat sattvic foods if you are taking a light vrat: fruits, milk, curd, nuts, sabudana, potato, singhare or kuttu atta and rock salt.
  • Keep a clean mind. Avoid anger, gossip, lies and harsh words.
  • Increase prayer. Read or listen to Vishnu Sahasranama, the Gita or stories of the Ekadashi, and do naam jap.
  • Do not sleep during the day, if possible, and keep the day simple.

Traditions differ widely – from strict nirjala (waterless) fasts to simple grain-free meals – so follow what your family or sampradaya does.

Who should not fast strictly?

Fasting is voluntary, and devotion matters more than severity. Children, the elderly, pregnant or nursing women, people with diabetes or other health conditions, and anyone on regular medicines should not fast strictly and should consult a doctor if in doubt. They can observe Ekadashi by eating light grain-free food, doing japa and keeping a calm mind.

Parana: how to break the fast

The fast is broken the next day, on Dwadashi, after sunrise and within the parana window given in the panchang. Do not break the fast before sunrise or after the Dwadashi tithi ends if you can help it. Start with a little water, tulsi leaf and a simple meal. In some traditions, feeding a brahmin, a cow or a needy person before eating is customary.

Naam jap on Ekadashi: what to chant

Any name of Vishnu or his avatars is suitable, and many devotees choose what they love:

  • Om Namo Bhagavate Vasudevaya – the twelve-syllable Vishnu mantra, loved by Vaishnavas.
  • Hare Krishna maha-mantra – see the Hare Krishna counter.
  • Shri Ram Jai Ram Jai Jai Ram – see the Ram naam jap counter.
  • Radhe Radhe – see the Radha naam jap counter.

A simple Ekadashi routine

  1. Wake in Brahma Muhurta if you can, bathe and take the vrat sankalp.
  2. Offer water, a lamp and tulsi leaves to Vishnu or Krishna.
  3. Chant one mala (108) of your chosen mantra.
  4. During the day, read a few verses of the Gita or listen to a kirtan.
  5. In the evening, do aarti, chant another mala and read the Ekadashi katha.
  6. Keep the lamp lit if your tradition does so, and sleep early.

A target of 3 or 5 malas on Ekadashi is common. The total time for 5 malas is about 30 minutes. Plan larger vows with the mantra calculator.

Devutthana Ekadashi and Tulsi Vivah

Devutthana (also called Prabodhini) Ekadashi in the month of Kartik is when Lord Vishnu is said to wake from his four-month yoga-nidra, so it marks the start of the auspicious season for weddings. Tulsi Vivah, the symbolic marriage of the tulsi plant with Shaligram or Krishna, is celebrated around this day. Read more on our Kartik maas naam jap page.

Mistakes to avoid on Ekadashi

  • Treating the fast as a punishment or a competition.
  • Skipping prayer and spending the day on screens while only avoiding grains.
  • Breaking the fast at the wrong time because of confusion about dates. Check your local panchang the day before.
  • Fasting strictly despite health problems.

Frequently asked questions

What are the Ekadashi dates in November 2026?

Rama Ekadashi is on 5 November and Devutthana (Prabodhini) Ekadashi on 20 November (Smarta) and 21 November (Vaishnava) 2026. Confirm with your local panchang.

What are the Ekadashi dates in December 2026?

Utpanna Ekadashi on 4 December and Mokshada Ekadashi on 20 December 2026.

Can I drink water on Ekadashi?

In most traditions yes, except for the strict nirjala vrat. Follow your family tradition and health needs.

Which mantra should I chant on Ekadashi?

Om Namo Bhagavate Vasudevaya, Hare Krishna maha-mantra, Ram naam or Radhe Radhe are all popular. Choose according to your tradition.

How many times should I chant on Ekadashi?

There is no fixed number. 108 is a common minimum and some chant 3, 5 or 11 malas on the day.
