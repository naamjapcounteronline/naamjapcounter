Hanuman Jayanti 2027 – Date, Puja Vidhi And Hanuman Mantra Jap

Hanuman Jayanti celebrates the birth of Hanuman, the symbol of strength and devotion. Here are the 2027 dates, a simple puja vidhi and a plan for Hanuman mantra jap and Chalisa recitation.

By Naam Jap Counter · Published November 8, 2026

When is Hanuman Jayanti celebrated in 2027?

In North India, Maharashtra, Gujarat, and across the majority of Indian states, Hanuman Jayanti 2027 falls on Tuesday, 20 April, coinciding with Chaitra Purnima (the full moon of Chaitra). Notably, the festival aligns with a Tuesday—the weekday traditionally dedicated to Hanuman worship. Drik Panchang records the Chaitra Purnima tithi as initiating at 4:51 AM IST on 20 April and concluding at 3:56 AM on 21 April.

Regional observances across the subcontinent follow distinct astronomical milestones:

  • Tamil Nadu & Kerala: Commemorated as Hanumath Jayanthi during the solar month of Margazhi on Amavasya (new moon) coupled with Moola nakshatra, falling on Thursday, 7 January 2027.
  • Karnataka & Parts of Maharashtra: Observed as Hanumad Vrata on Margashira Shukla Trayodashi during December.
  • Andhra Pradesh & Telangana: Celebrated through a 41-day deeksha concluding on Vaishakha Krishna Dashami.

Observe the festival according to the traditions of your ancestral lineage or regional temple community.

Traditional temple rituals and festive puja vidhi

  1. Brahma Muhurta snana: Arise before dawn, bathe, and dress in auspicious red or deep saffron garments.
  2. Shrine preparation: Cleanse the puja altar and respectfully arrange an idol or portrait of Lord Hanuman, ideally flanked by Shri Sita-Rama.
  3. Sacred anointing (Chola samarpan): In temples and traditional shrines, priests apply pure red vermilion (sindoor) blended with aromatic jasmine or sesame oil (Chameli tel), commemorating Hanuman's total devotion to Rama.
  4. Floral & food offerings: Present fragrant garlands of fresh Calotropis (Aak/Madar flowers), betel leaves (Paan beeda), and offerings of yellow gram flour Boondi, besan laddoos, or jaggery paired with roasted gram.
  5. Lighting the deepa: Ignite an earthen lamp filled with mustard or sesame oil, infusing the atmosphere with meditative warmth.
  6. Arati & festive distribution: Sing the time-honored "Aarti Kije Hanuman Lala Ki" and distribute sacred prasad among community members and spiritual seekers.

Organizing an anushthan: Hanuman Chalisa vows

On Hanuman Jayanti, collective groups and solitary devotees frequently undertake structured recitation vows of the forty chaupais composed by Mahakavi Tulsidas. Below is a structured planning schedule:

Festive japa schedule for the celebratory day

Pair textual chanting with silent name repetition. Begin the day with 1 mala (108 repetitions) of Om Hanumate Namah during dawn hours. Spend the afternoon in spiritual contemplation or selfless community service (Seva). In the evening, gather for community Chalisa recitation and conclude with companion rounds on our Ram naam jap counter, as traditional lore teaches that honoring Rama brings the deepest joy to Hanuman.

Frequently asked questions

What is the date of Hanuman Jayanti in 2027?

In North India and most of India it is Tuesday, 20 April 2027 (Chaitra Purnima). Tamil Nadu observes it on 7 January 2027, and Karnataka and Andhra Pradesh follow different dates.

Why are there different dates for Hanuman Jayanti?

Different regions follow different traditions and calendars about Hanuman's birth, so the festival is observed on different tithis.

How many times should I chant Om Hanumate Namah?

Commonly 108 times a day, or 11, 21 or 51 times as a vow.
