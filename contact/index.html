Contact FAQ

How fast do you reply?

We read every message and try to reply within a few working days.

Can you add a new mantra to the counter?

Yes, please email the mantra in Devanagari with transliteration and, if you can, a short meaning and the tradition it belongs to.

I found a mistake in a mantra or meaning. What should I do?

Please email us the page link and the correction. We take mantra accuracy seriously and will review it.

My counts disappeared. Can you restore them?

Counts are stored only in your own browser, so we cannot restore them if browser data was cleared. We do not keep your data on any server.

Do you give spiritual or medical advice?

No. For initiation and personal spiritual guidance please approach your guru or tradition, and for health concerns consult a qualified professional.
